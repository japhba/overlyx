01 / Question and model

How long does a spatial pattern survive?

A field diffuses on a periodic one-dimensional domain. Follow one Fourier component at a time.

\[\partial_t u=D\,\partial_{xx}u\]

\[A_k(t)=A_k(0)e^{-Dk^2t}\]

Constant D · no sources · no advection

02 / Result

Double the frequency. Four times faster decay.

\[\tau_k=\frac{1}{Dk^2}\]

Small-scale structure disappears first.

03 / Scope

The conclusion depends on the model assumptions

What follows

For constant diffusivity, the mode lifetime falls as the inverse square of spatial frequency.

What to check

Boundary conditions, spatially varying diffusivity and nonlinear coupling may change the mode dynamics.

In measured data, estimate lifetimes with uncertainty and inspect residuals.

04 / Takeaway and reproducibility

Spatial scale predicts the lifetime of a pattern

Higher-frequency modes decay sooner under diffusion.

The prediction is quantitative: τ₂ = τ₁ / 4 when k₂ = 2k₁.

Reproduce the plot: evaluate exp(−s) and exp(−4s) at 101 equally spaced values of s from 0 to 3.
