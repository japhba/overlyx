Abstract

Sunlight is scattered by the molecules of the air with an intensity proportional to \(\lambda^{-4}\). Short wavelengths are therefore scattered far more than long ones, which colours the daytime sky blue and the sunset red.

Result

\[ \frac{I_{450\,\mathrm{nm}}}{I_{700\,\mathrm{nm}}} = \left(\frac{700}{450}\right)^{4} \approx 5.9 \]

Blue light is scattered about six times as strongly as red.
