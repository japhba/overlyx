Why the sky is blue

Question

Sunlight contains every colour. Why does the daytime sky look blue, and the sunset red?

Answer in one line

Air molecules scatter light with an intensity that grows steeply as the wavelength shrinks:

\[ I \propto \lambda^{-4} \]

Scattered intensity across the spectrum

From violet (380 nm, left) to red (700 nm, right): blue light is scattered about six times as strongly as red, \((700/450)^4 \approx 5.9\).

Why sunsets are red

  • Near the horizon, sunlight crosses up to 40 times more air
  • Most of the blue is scattered out of the beam on the way
