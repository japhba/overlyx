TeX engines in the browser (/texwasm/): pdfTeX, LuaHBTeX and XeTeX with xdvipdfmx, bibtex8 and makeindex,
compiled to WebAssembly from the sources of TeX Live 2023.0 with BusyTeX's build. They build the PDFs of
end-to-end encrypted projects in the browser (packages/client/src/e2e/compile.ts).

How they are built: tex-wasm/README.md in OverLyX's repository (https://github.com/japhba/overlyx).
The engines, their SHA-256 and their complete corresponding source: the GitHub release named in
tex-wasm/release.json.
