Fonts of the OverLyX editor (Settings ▸ Editor ▸ Text font / Math font)
========================================================================

scripts/build-editor-fonts.py, under family names of its own. Each file keeps its copyright and licence
notices. The licences: SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL.txt below, https://openfontlicense.org), GUST Font
License (Latin Modern, TeX Gyre, New Computer Modern: https://www.gust.org.pl/projects/e-foundry/licenses),
Bitstream Charter and Bitstream Vera licences (XCharter, DejaVu), CC BY 4.0 (Luciole).
